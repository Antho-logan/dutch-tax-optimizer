# 🇳🇱 Dutch Tax Optimizer

**Keep More of What You Earn. Automatically.**

AI-powered tax optimization for Dutch ZZP and freelancers. Stop overpaying taxes and start saving thousands.

---

## 🎯 Market Opportunity

**Target:** 1.1M Dutch ZZP (freelancers) (+15% YoY growth)

**Problem:**
- 2026 tax changes: Zelfstandigenaftrek slashed from €2,470 → €1,200
- Complex Dutch tax system (KvK, BSN, loonbelasting, inkomstenbelasting)
- Most ZZPers overpay €2,000-5,000/year in taxes

**Solution:**
- AI-powered receipt scanning (Dutch stores)
- Every ZZP deduction, automatically found
- WBSO wizard (Dutch R&D tax credit up to €18,828)
- Real-time tax projections

**Market Size:** €1.1B potential savings for Dutch freelancers

---

## ✨ Features

- **📸 Receipt AI** - Snap a photo, extract deductions in 2 seconds (50+ Dutch stores)
- **🔍 Smart Deductions** - Every Dutch freelance deduction, auto-discovered
- **🚀 WBSO Wizard** - Dutch R&D tax credit made simple
- **📊 Tax Projection** - See your tax future before it happens
- **💶 BV vs ZZP Comparison** - Which structure saves you more?
- **📄 PDF Export** - Professional summaries for your accountant

```bash

npm start
```

Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.
